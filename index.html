3 Select Prompting Strategy

Choose one or more prompting strategies to test. Each strategy will generate separate results, allowing you to compare their effectiveness.

💡 Multiple Strategy Testing:
Selecting multiple strategies will run simulations for each approach independently. You'll receive comparative results showing which strategy produces the most accurate/consistent predictions. Useful for research validation and methodology comparison.
Direct Persona Embedding Recommended
Directly embeds age and personality characteristics into the prompt. Simple and effective.
Chain-of-Thought Reasoning
Asks the LLM to explicitly reason through how a person with given characteristics would evaluate. More detailed but slower.
Comparative Evaluation
Asks the LLM to compare how different personas might react differently. Useful for understanding persona effects.
📝 Prompt Preview: Direct Persona Embedding
System Instructions:
You are participating in a research study evaluating AI explanations. Your responses should reflect your age and personality characteristics authentically.
Persona (Example: Age 25, High Openness):
You are a 25-year-old person with high openness to new experiences. You enjoy exploring new ideas, appreciate abstract thinking, and are curious about complex explanations. You are comfortable with technology and tend to engage deeply with detailed information.
Task:
Question: Is this comment toxic? AI Explanation: This comment was flagged as toxic because it contains personal insults ("idiot") and uses aggressive, demeaning language. The model identified these linguistic patterns as strongly associated with inappropriate communication based on training data from thousands of labeled examples. Evaluation: Based on your age and personality, please rate the following on a scale of 1-10: 1. Understanding: How well do you understand WHY the AI made this decision? (1 = Don't understand at all, 10 = Completely understand) 2. Trust: How much do you trust this AI decision? (1 = Don't trust at all, 10 = Completely trust) 3. Satisfaction: How satisfied are you with this explanation? (1 = Very unsatisfied, 10 = Very satisfied) Response Format: Provide only numerical scores (1-10) for each metric.
Example LLM Response:
Understanding: 8 Trust: 7 Satisfaction: 8 Reasoning: As someone who is open to new ideas and comfortable with detailed explanations, I found this explanation clear and well-justified. The reference to training data provides good context. The concrete example ("idiot") makes it easy to understand why the comment was flagged. My understanding and satisfaction are high, though my trust is slightly lower because I'd want to know more about potential false positives.
💡 Note: The actual prompt will be customized for each of the 9 persona combinations (3 age groups × 3 openness levels) and repeated for each explanation in your dataset.
🎯 Strategy Comparison:
• Direct Persona: Fast, consistent, good for large batches (Recommended for most users)
• Chain-of-Thought: More detailed reasoning, better for understanding why scores differ
• Comparative: Explicitly models persona differences, useful for research validation
