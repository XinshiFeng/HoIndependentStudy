3 Select Prompting Strategy

Choose one or more prompting strategies to test. Each strategy will generate separate results, allowing you to compare their effectiveness.

💡 Multiple Strategy Testing:
Selecting multiple strategies will run simulations for each approach independently. You'll receive comparative results showing which strategy produces the most accurate/consistent predictions.
Direct Persona Embedding Recommended
Directly embeds age and personality characteristics into the prompt. Simple and effective.
System Instructions:
You are participating in a research study evaluating AI explanations. Your responses should reflect your age and personality characteristics authentically.
Persona (Example: Age 25, High Openness):
You are a 25-year-old person with high openness to new experiences. You enjoy exploring new ideas, appreciate abstract thinking, and are curious about complex explanations.
Task:
Question: Is this comment toxic? AI Explanation: This comment was flagged as toxic because it contains personal insults. Evaluation: Rate Understanding and Trust on a scale of 1-10.
Scenario-Based Simulation Experimental
Places the LLM in a realistic scenario context (e.g., "You're reviewing this at work..."). May increase engagement simulation.
Scenario Context:
You are at work, reviewing content moderation decisions made by an AI system. As a 25-year-old team member with high openness to new ideas, you're tasked with evaluating whether the AI's explanations are clear and trustworthy.
The Explanation:
Question: Is this comment toxic? AI's Explanation: Flagged due to personal insults and aggressive language. Rate your understanding and trust (1-10 scale).
Chain-of-Thought Reasoning
Asks the LLM to explicitly reason through evaluation. More detailed but slower.
Chain-of-Thought Instructions:
As a 25-year-old person with high openness, think through step-by-step: Step 1: Consider your characteristics (age, personality) Step 2: Read the explanation carefully Step 3: Reflect on what makes sense Step 4: Provide ratings with reasoning
Explanation to Evaluate:
Comment flagged as toxic due to personal insults. Provide Understanding and Trust ratings (1-10) with your reasoning.
Comparative Evaluation
Explicitly models how different personas react differently. Useful for research validation.
Comparative Analysis:
Consider how people with different characteristics might evaluate this differently: Younger (25, high openness): How would they respond? Older (65, low openness): How might it differ? Provide ratings for your specific persona (25, high openness).
Explanation:
Comment flagged as toxic due to personal insults. Rate Understanding and Trust (1-10).
